# Dataset attribution

This repository uses National Energy System Operator (NESO), Historic Demand Data, annual CSVs for 2022, 2023, 2024 and 2025.

Source: https://www.neso.energy/data-portal/historic-demand-data

Licence: National Energy SO Open Data Licence v1.0, linked from the source dataset. See https://www.neso.energy/data-portal/neso-open-licence and check the source page for current terms.

**Supported by National Energy SO Open Data.** Neither the analysis nor the forecasts are endorsed by NESO.

Raw source files are downloaded locally and excluded from Git. The reports contain derived results and retain source attribution. The source-data licence is separate from the MIT licence applying to original project code.
