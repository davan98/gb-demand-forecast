How to interpret these results

Training: 2022–2023 after lag warm-up. Validation: 2024. Test: 2025. The model stays fixed during the test year. Features use demand at least 28 days old because this public historic feed normally arrives 21 days late.

This is a backtest using retrospectively corrected data, not an operational forecasting service. Archived publication vintages are unavailable. Weather and holiday effects are not explicitly modelled. The uncertainty band undercovers in 2025 and needs further work.
